KOTR POSTER MAKER
=================
Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome or Edge. Nothing to install.
Everything runs in your browser; photos and posters never leave your PC.

Two ways to make a poster
-------------------------
REAL POSTER (default)
  1. Save real fight posters you like (UFC, PFL, Rizin, AEW, boxing, whatever)
     into the "posters" folder next to this file. Any JPG/PNG works.
  2. In the app, "Add folder" -> pick the posters folder (or "Add posters" for single files).
     They are stored inside the browser, so you only do this once.
  3. Click a poster to use it, or press "Best fit for these fighters" to let the
     app rank the library by colour match, shape and darkness for your two fighters.
  4. The original fighters stay underneath. Put the KOTR fighters over them with the
     Size / Move / Mirror sliders, then raise "Blur" and "Darken" until the originals
     disappear. "Cover top" / "Cover bottom" hide the original title and names.
  5. Text uses the poster's own accent colour (untick "Use the poster's own accent
     colour" to use the KOTR gold instead).

KOTR DESIGN
  The built-in black-and-gold layout, no source poster needed.

Fighters
  - Transparent PNGs drop straight in.
  - Plain screenshot? Click the background colour on the thumbnail, adjust
    Tolerance / Soft edge, press "Apply colour cut-out". Or use "AI cut-out"
    (needs internet, downloads a model the first time).

Sponsors: add logos (transparent PNG best). They sit in the bottom strip.
Export: "Download PNG (2x quality)" or "Copy to clipboard" and paste into Discord.
Projects: "Save project (.json)" keeps photos + text so you can reload later.
Fonts (Anton, Bebas Neue, Oswald) load from Google Fonts, so the first open needs internet.
